Wriggly Snake

Go get it

  1. 1Count on the deal: 13 − needed tiles held − 1 for a pair.
  2. 2Chase it at 4 steps or fewer. At 5, only with a half-flush backup.
  3. 3Throw other suits, then dragons, then extra copies.
  4. 4All four of a needed tile gone? Switch to the half flush.
